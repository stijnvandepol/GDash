# P2000 widget — design

Date: 16 October 2026. Status: approved in conversation, awaiting review of
this spec.

## Goal

Show what the emergency services are called out for near home: a P2000
widget that lists the latest alerts (fire, police, KNRM, trauma helicopter,
optionally ambulance) for one *veiligheidsregio* or for one to five places,
as a calm "what is happening around here" tile.

Success: a household in, say, Eindhoven picks "Brabant-Zuidoost" or the
places "Eindhoven, Veldhoven", sees the fire and police call-outs of the last
hours within about two minutes of the alert, without an account or key.

Not in scope: a radius around home (the source cannot do it), ambulance
priority filters, capcodes, announcements or sounds for new alerts, an own
radio receiver.

## Source

[alarmeringen.nl](https://www.alarmeringen.nl/webfeeds.html) (DPG Media)
publishes RSS 2.0 feeds, checked on 16 October 2026:

| Feed | URL | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Region and discipline | `https://alarmeringen.nl/feeds/region/{region}/{discipline}.rss` | up to 100 items; for fire a few weeks |
| Place | `https://alarmeringen.nl/feeds/city/{place}.rss` | up to 50 items, all disciplines |

- `{discipline}` is `brandweer`, `ambulance`, `politie`, `knrm` or `trauma`.
  An unknown region, place or discipline answers 404.
- Each item has `title` (the raw pager text), `description` (a readable
  sentence such as "Woningbrand op Franz Lisztstraat in Hengelo"),
  `pubDate`, a `guid`, and a `link` whose path is
  `/{province}/{region}/{place}/{id}/p2000-….html`. The place slug in that
  path is what GDash filters on.
- Answers carry `Last-Modified` and `Cache-Control: max-age=98`.
- Licence: CC BY-NC-ND 3.0. GDash credits the source on the tile and in the
  docs, shows the items unchanged (only filtered and shortened to fit), and is
  used non-commercially by whoever runs it. The guide says so.

The 25 region slugs, all answering 200: `groningen`, `friesland`,
`drenthe`, `ijsselland`, `twente`, `noord-en-oost-gelderland`,
`gelderland-midden`, `gelderland-zuid`, `utrecht`, `noord-holland-noord`,
`zaanstreek-waterland`, `kennemerland`, `amsterdam-amstelland`,
`gooi-en-vechtstreek`, `haaglanden`, `hollands-midden`, `rotterdam-rijnmond`,
`zuid-holland-zuid`, `zeeland`, `midden-en-west-brabant`, `brabant-noord`,
`brabant-zuidoost`, `limburg-noord`, `limburg-zuid`, `flevoland`.

## How it works

Everything is fetched as region and discipline feeds, also for places: a
place's feed has no discipline filter, but its region's discipline feeds do,
and their items name the place in the link. So one place costs no extra
request when its region is already fetched, and the discipline filter works
the same in both modes.

1. **Choosing places.** In the editor, the admin types a place and chooses
   **Test**. The server fetches `feeds/city/{slug}.rss` once and answers the
   place's slug, display name and region, read from the item links. 404 means
   unknown ("Check the spelling, as alarmeringen.nl writes it, such as
   Hengelo (Ov)"). A place without any items has no region to read, and the
   answer says to choose its veiligheidsregio instead. The place field is
   prefilled with the name of the weather location.
2. **Fetching.** The server collects the distinct `(region, discipline)`
   feeds that all dashboards need, at most 20 (`maxP2000Feeds`). It fetches
   them every 2 minutes with conditional requests, keeps the newest 100
   items of each feed, and publishes them under the data key
   `p2000.default` as `{"feeds": {"<region>/<discipline>": [alert…]},
   "skipped": ["<region>/<discipline>"…]}`. Any failing feed fails the
   whole refresh, as with news feeds: the screens keep the last complete
   set and show it as stale instead of quietly losing a region.
3. **Showing.** The screen takes the feeds of its widget, keeps the items of
   the chosen places (or all of the region), drops items older than the
   widget's hours, removes duplicates by `guid`, sorts newest first and shows
   the lines that fit.

An alert as the server publishes it:

```json
{"id": "f63426ebe71bd895aa5449c1b9e5d1d0", "at": "2026-10-16T10:14:04Z",
 "discipline": "brandweer", "place": "spijkenisse",
 "text": "Woningbrand op Nieuwstraat in Spijkenisse"}
```

## Widget

Type `p2000`, minimum 6 × 4, title "P2000" (or the region or places).

| Setting | Kind | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `region` | `choice` of the 25 slugs | either `region` or `places`, not both, not neither |
| `places` | `p2000Places`, list of `{name, slug, region}` | 1 to 5; slug `^[a-z0-9-]{1,60}$`, region one of the 25 |
| `disciplines` | `choices` of the five | at least 1; default all but `ambulance` |
| `hours` | `integer` | 1 to 48, default 12 |
| `maxItems` | `integer` | 1 to 20, default 8 |

A row shows a small mark in the colour of the discipline (red for fire,
blue for police, green for ambulance, orange for KNRM and trauma
helicopter), the readable sentence, and how long ago it happened ("12 min",
"3 h"). The tile ends with the credit "Alarmeringen.nl". If there are no
alerts in the window, the tile says "Nothing in the last 12 hours". The
title turns to the warning colour when the data is more than one interval
plus a minute old, as with departures.

## Code layout

Following Weather warnings and Departures:

- `internal/p2000/`
  - `regions.go`: the 25 regions with slug and name.
  - `client.go`: fetching with conditional requests, a 2 MB answer limit,
    and the GDash User-Agent.
  - `parse.go`: a small RSS decoder of its own, because the parser in
    `internal/news` keeps neither `description` nor `guid`. It reuses that
    package's date and plain-text helpers by exporting them, and reads the
    place slug from the link path.
  - `place.go`: the place test.
  - Tests with `testdata/` feeds; no network.
- `internal/display/p2000.go`: the widget type, `p2000Places` validation,
  and `P2000Feeds(dashboards)`. Registered in `layout.go` and narrowed per
  screen in `entities.go`.
- `cmd/gdash/dashboardsources.go`: the `p2000.default` source with the
  interval and cap constants; an e2e stand-in path in the test upstream.
- `internal/api/p2000.go`: `POST /api/p2000-place-tests` `{name}` → `200
  {name, slug, region, regionName}`; `422` for an invalid name, `502` with
  `place_unknown`, `place_without_alerts` or `fetch_failed`.
- Web:
  - `widgets/P2000.svelte` + `p2000.ts` (filtering, sorting, age texts) with
    tests, dispatched in `App.svelte`, waiting texts in `waiting.ts`.
  - Admin: palette entry in `widgetSettings.ts`, a form of its own,
    `P2000WidgetSettings.svelte` (as Traffic has: region and places exclude
    each other), with the Test button, the discipline checkboxes and the
    numbers, and a built-in card in `integrationRegistry.ts`.
  - Strings in English, Dutch, German and French.

## Errors and limits

- Validation happens on the server at save time. A widget with both or
  neither of region and places is refused with a field error.
- Every request has a timeout (15 s) and answers above 2 MB are refused.
  Failures are logged per feed without the item contents. The tile keeps the
  last items and shows them as stale.
- More than 20 feeds in total: the first 20 by sorted key are fetched, and
  the rest are listed under `skipped` and logged. A widget with a skipped feed
  shows "Too many P2000 areas on the dashboards" instead of a list that is
  quietly incomplete.
- The place test is limited like the other upstream tests.

## Testing

- Go: parsing (readable text, dates, place slug, a broken item skipped),
  conditional requests, a failing feed failing the refresh, the feed cap,
  widget validation, place test answers (found, 404, no items), and screen
  narrowing.
- Web: filtering by place, discipline and hours; deduplication; age texts;
  the empty and stale states.
- E2E: `web/e2e/NN-p2000.spec.ts` against the stand-in upstream. It adds the
  widget with a place via the Test button and checks the tile in the preview.
